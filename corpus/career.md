### Moonfare: AI professionalisation automation

Edgar automated Moonfare's regulated investor professionalisation journey (part of KYC and AML) for DACH, keeping a human in the loop for any decision that affects an investor record. That design choice is what let it ship into a regulated process despite imperfect model accuracy. He built it as a modular service that can be triggered from different touchpoints, the first being onboarding.

He built it with n8n, AWS Bedrock and Claude Code, 10 to 15 times faster than a conventional non-AI delivery approach. It saves about $250K a year.

The demo audience included the CEO, along with the CTO, the Chief Transformation Officer and the AI steering committee, and it was then shown to the whole company. The CEO asked to share it as the example of how operations teams should eventually automate their own processes once the company's AI transformation is complete.

## How Edgar works

Edgar describes his process as five stages: discover, define, design, deliver and deduce. Discovery covers customer interviews, data analysis and market research; definition narrows to the user problem with journey maps, jobs to be done and acceptance criteria; design validates solutions through prototypes and user tests; delivery ships as beta, minimum viable feature or phased rollout; deduction measures results with experiments and dashboards.

He groups his strengths into four areas: product strategy, product execution, customer insight and influencing people, with adjacent depth in technology, product design and marketing. He doesn't like repetitive work, so he builds systems, automates and sets operating models that cut communication overhead.
